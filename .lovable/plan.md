# Smart Irrigation Website Plan

## Direction
Build the selected **Eco-precision luxury** direction as a responsive, multi-page company website using the chosen **Forest & Water palette**, with polished engineering-focused typography and real irrigation/landscape imagery.

The website will use the actual company name **Smart Irrigation Design & Consulting**, not the placeholder prototype brand. Copy will be professionally rewritten from the PDF for clarity while preserving verified company facts.

## Pages
1. **Home**
   - Premium irrigation hero with direct call and WhatsApp actions
   - Established 2017, up to 50% water savings, Rain Bird expertise, South India coverage
   - Featured services, work process, selected client/project names, founder credibility, and inquiry CTA
2. **Services**
   - Landscape drip, sprinkler, pop-up, and automated irrigation
   - Agriculture, horticulture, plantation, golf, and sports-field irrigation
   - Fountains, water features, fog systems, koi ponds, and swimming pools
   - Rainwater harvesting, consulting, installation, and AMC maintenance
3. **Projects**
   - Selected project timeline from 2017–2026, including Jayabheri, Aparna, Hetero, Raheja, JLL, Hallmark, Lansum, Radisson Hotel, SAS, and other profile-listed work
   - Avoid unsupported project statistics or invented testimonials
4. **About**
   - Company story, objectives, founder K. Santharam, technical team, product expertise, and customer-service approach
5. **Contact**
   - Phone, email, Hyderabad office address, service-area summary, and prominent call/WhatsApp links

## Visual Implementation
- Preserve the selected prototype’s high-impact hero, metric band, visual service grid, dark green inquiry section, and restrained premium footer
- Define all forest, water, cream, typography, and surface values as semantic design tokens
- Load the selected display/body fonts correctly in the document head
- Generate original, realistic imagery for landscape irrigation, drip systems, fountains/pools, and rainwater harvesting; no external hotlinked images
- Responsive navigation and mobile sticky inquiry controls with accessible contrast and touch targets

## Recommendations Included
- Position the company as a specialist in **irrigation engineering, supply, installation, and maintenance**, rather than claiming unsupported full-service landscaping construction
- Lead with water savings, technical design, international product expertise, lifecycle support, and regional project experience
- Add an explicit process: site analysis → design and quotation → procurement → installation → commissioning → AMC support
- Use project names and dates as trust evidence, while excluding invented case-study results
- Keep inquiries frictionless through call, email, and WhatsApp links without introducing a backend form

## SEO and Quality
- Unique titles and descriptions for every page
- One clear H1 per page, semantic sections, descriptive image alt text, local-business structured data, and relevant Hyderabad/Telangana service language
- Validate navigation, responsive behavior, external actions, and final visual composition on desktop and mobile
